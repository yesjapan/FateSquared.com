Synopsis

Downtown San Diego. A fighter buried in debt. A company that will not stop calling.

Ever since his MMA fighting career ended at its height with a horrible injury, George Williams has worked every day of the week to pay off a crushing debt from the medical bills as a personal trainer in the heart of downtown San Diego.

Things are slowly getting back on track until a lucrative training offer from LETR, a shadowy, omnipresent tech corporation tempts him with a life-altering paycheck. He's ignored the request three times, but now the offer is too good to refuse.

His girlfriend, Shin Hye Kim, a sharp, relentless public relations agent, has her doubts about the company and the job. It all seems too good to be true and LETR's past makes her uneasy. She warns him not to go.

But he doesn't listen…

Now, it may be too late.
